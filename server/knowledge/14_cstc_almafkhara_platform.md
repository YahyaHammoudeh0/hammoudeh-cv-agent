# CSTC and Almafkhara — Multi-Tenant Training Platform (May 2026 to Present)

Mohammad Yahya Hammoudeh is a freelance Full-Stack and AI Engineer for two Saudi training centers: Creativity Skills Training Center (CSTC) and Almafkhara Academy (معهد أكاديمية المفخرة العالي للتدريب). He has served both clients since May 2026.

It is one white-label, multi-tenant platform built with Next.js and Supabase. Each tenant gets its own runtime identity, theme, content, features, and its own Supabase project, while sharing one canonical database schema and one application build. The two live production sites are:
- CSTC: https://cstc.edu.sa — Arabic-first training center site and operations platform.
- Almafkhara Academy: https://almafkhara.com.sa — independent deep-teal brand with a bespoke editorial landing page.

The platform spans an Arabic-first CRM and LMS, finance and accounting, HR and trainer payouts, reporting, certificates, and a WhatsApp AI assistant with voice-note transcription and human handoff. Arabic RTL and English are treated as equal first-class experiences, not translations bolted on later.

The codebase is private, so the public proof is the two live production sites used daily by the training centers.
