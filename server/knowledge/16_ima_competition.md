## The case
West Valley Fresh Distribution Company (Southern Central Valley, California) wanted to decide whether to enter the organic Hass avocado market. Local farms could supply 20,000 organic avocados at $0.20 each by June 2026. The team analysed 10+ years of Hass Avocado Board data (43,840 weekly observations across 40 US cities) to answer: should West Valley enter, and if so, which five cities should it target?

## What they did
- Cost-volume-profit (CVP) analysis per city: local organic retail price drives wholesale revenue, shipping cost by distance drives expense.
- Price-elasticity analysis: a moderate inverse relationship between price and volume, but price alone does not explain demand.
- Sensitivity analysis: the top-five ranking stayed stable under ±50% shipping-cost scenarios.
- Forecasting models as a validation check on the ranking.
- Market findings: Los Angeles had the largest total avocado sales every year (2019 to 2025H1); Seattle led organic market share in 5 of 7 years; Pacific Northwest cities showed the strongest organic preference.

## Recommendation
Enter the market. Target Seattle, Boise, Portland, Spokane and San Diego. Shipping is material but not large enough to change the top-five ranking.
